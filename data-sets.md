## Reading Guidance

- Use the grouped exploit page when tracking a specific library/version pair.
- Use the safety-ranked page when deciding what to deploy or avoid.
- Treat unknown/null hook lines as requiring validation in your own game build.

## Caveats

- This data is version-sensitive.
- Practical impact depends on context, object type, and workflow.
